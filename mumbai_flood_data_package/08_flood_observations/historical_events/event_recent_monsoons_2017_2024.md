# Synthesis of Recent Major Mumbai Flood Events (2017–2024)

## 1. Event: 29 August 2017 (315 mm in 24 Hours)
- **Description**: Severe cyclonic depression over the Arabian Sea caused intense rain exceeding 80 mm/hour during afternoon peak.
- **Flooding Extent**: Dadar, Hindmata, Parel, and King's Circle submerged up to 1.2 m. Suburban rail networks (Central, Western, Harbour) completely shut down by 14:00 IST.
- **Fatalities**: 14, including prominent medical doctors who drowned in uncovered stormwater manholes during flood conditions.

## 2. Event: 2-3 July 2019 (375 mm in 24 Hours)
- **Description**: Heaviest 24-hour rainfall in July since 2005.
- **Impact**: Compound wall collapse in Malad East claiming 31 lives; Milan and Andheri subways completely inundated under 2 meters of water.

## 3. Event: 5 August 2020 (331 mm in South Mumbai with 106 km/h Gale Winds)
- **Description**: Rare cyclonic surge focusing heavy precipitation on Island City (Colaba: 331 mm; Santacruz: 162 mm).
- **Tidal Surge**: 4.6m high tide combined with 106 km/h gale force winds pushed seawater over Marine Drive tetrapods into residential colonies.

## 4. Event: 18 July 2021 (Chembur / Vikhroli Cloudburst - 253 mm in 4 Hours)
- **Description**: Localized night-time cloudburst between 00:00 and 04:00 IST causing urban flash floods and devastating hillock landslides in Bharat Nagar (Chembur) and Vikhroli.

## 5. Event: 25 September 2024 (Late Monsoon Deluge - 212 mm in 5 Hours)
- **Description**: Post-monsoon convective storm paralyzed Mumbai during evening rush hour; red alert issued as local trains stalled and Kurla station waterlogged.
