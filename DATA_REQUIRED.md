## Status rules

Files present in the workspace are not automatically verified observations. Their metadata and source claims have not been checked against the originating agency. Until lineage, license, spatial/temporal semantics and file validity are checked, label them **UNVERIFIED** in the audit and do not publish their numeric values as measured/official. `UNVERIFIED` is a review status, not a sixth data-origin class. Once reviewed, assign exactly one origin class: **MEASURED / REAL**, **PUBLIC DATA**, **DERIVED**, **ASSUMED**, or **SYNTHETIC**. Preserve both origin class and verification status in every dataset release.

Large rasters remain files/object-store assets; PostGIS should store their extent, CRS, resolution, checksum, source and URI, not the raster bytes.
